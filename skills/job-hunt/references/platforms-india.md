# Job platforms in India and how to reach them

LinkedIn and Naukri, the two biggest sources, block automated reading. Their **job-alert emails** in the candidate's inbox (or a browser session where the candidate is logged in) are the dependable way in.

## Search directly (public pages, no login)
| Platform | Best for | How |
|---|---|---|
| Indeed India (in.indeed.com) | Large volume across all major cities | Web search or a connected Indeed tool; use the "Posted on" date |
| Weekday (weekday.works) | Curated PM / PO / BA roles at product companies and startups, with "x days ago" and direct links | Open `weekday.works/jobs/product-manager-jobs-in-india`, `/product-owner-jobs-in-india`, `/business-analyst-jobs-in-india` |
| Company career pages | Target employers | Search "<company> careers product manager", open the page, confirm the role is live and recent |
| Aggregators (Jooble, Careerjet, Mployee, Hyring) | Reposts from many sites | Include only after opening the page and confirming the role is live |

## Through job-alert emails (set up once, read daily)
Set a daily alert for each target role in each target city; the assistant reads the emails.
| Platform | Best for |
|---|---|
| LinkedIn Jobs | Largest source of PM/PO roles; also recruiter "hiring" posts |
| Naukri | India's largest portal; strong for BA, delivery and BFSI roles |
| Instahyre | Product companies and startups; matches on salary band |
| Indeed alerts | Daily matches from your Indeed profile |
| iimjobs | Mid-senior roles at consulting firms and product companies |
| Foundit (Monster India) | General portal; BFSI and IT services |
| Hirist | Tech roles, including product and technical BA |
| Cutshort | Startup and tech roles matched on skills |
| Wellfound | Startup roles with salary shown upfront |
| Talent500 | Global capability centres (GCCs) hiring in India |
| Glassdoor | Listings plus salary and interview reviews |
| Shine, TimesJobs | General portals |
| Hirect | Chat directly with startup founders and hiring managers |
| Uplers | Remote roles with overseas companies |

## Usually not useful for experienced professionals
Internshala, Freshersworld, Unstop (internships and fresher roles); Apna, WorkIndia (mostly frontline and entry-level); National Career Service (government portal, mostly entry-level).

## Reading alert emails
- Search the last 3 days, including archived mail (inbox-cleanup rules often archive alerts).
- Open each alert and take each job's own URL from the body; clean tracking parameters.
- Only count a job as new if its own posting date is within 3 days.
- Read only. Don't label, archive or reply to anything.
