# Compensation in India: talking about CTC

## Terms
- **CTC:** total annual cost to the company, including employer PF, gratuity and variable pay. In-hand pay is usually 70–80% of fixed CTC divided by 12.
- **Fixed vs variable:** always confirm what share is variable and how it's paid.
- **LPA:** lakhs per annum (₹10 LPA = ₹10,00,000 a year).

## Typical hikes on a switch
Roughly 30–40% over current CTC is common; 50%+ happens for in-demand skills, a strong title jump, or when the current pay is clearly below market. A much larger ask is fine to aim for, but tell the user it can filter them out at screening, where expected CTC fields are often used as hard cut-offs.

## Market ranges
Ranges vary a lot by company type. Look them up for the specific role, city and experience level (AmbitionBox, Glassdoor, PayScale, levels.fyi, recent offers in job posts) rather than relying on memory, and quote the source. As a pattern: product companies, BFSI in-house teams and global capability centres usually pay well above IT services firms for the same title.

## Answering CTC questions in applications
- Current CTC: the real figure. Background checks verify it.
- Expected CTC: a range whose bottom is the user's real minimum. "Negotiable" is fine for a first screen if their expectation is far above the typical hike.
- Notice period: the real figure; mention buyout or early release only if the user confirms it's possible.
